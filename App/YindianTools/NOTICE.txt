The analysis algorithms and stylesheet are retained from upstream. The result
renderer keeps all matching rows instead of silently capping the table at 600. The iOS app
provides a read-only local database bridge and native comparison map. index.html
also loads interface.js to translate interface controls from the app's independent
text catalogs; source character, language and pronunciation data is not translated.
